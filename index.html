Privacy Policy for NekoNote

Effective Date: November 6, 2026
Application: NekoNote (Package ID: www.aftonstudio.nekonote)
Developer: Afton Studio
Privacy Commitment Summary: NekoNote is built with a privacy-first, local-first architecture. We do not operate external data tracking servers, we do not sell or monetize your personal information, and we do not display third-party advertisements. Your notes, voice memos, checklists, and documents reside strictly on your own device unless you explicitly choose to back them up to your own private Google Drive account.

1. Local-First Data Storage (Your Data Stays With You)

NekoNote operates as an offline-first productivity tool. All data you create within the app:

  • Notes & Checklists: Stored locally in a secure Room database on your device storage.
  • Voice Memos & Audio: Stored strictly inside your app-specific internal private storage directory.
  • Images & Document Attachments: Handled locally; files you import remain securely stored on your device.
  • Habits & Mood Logs: Stored locally on your device with no external transmission.

2. Device Permissions and Why We Need Them

NekoNote requests runtime permissions only when you access specific features, following the principle of least privilege:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively when you take a photo to attach to a note, scan text via on-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR), or scan a QR code for peer-to-peer offline note sharing. Camera data is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted externally.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used strictly when you tap the record button to capture voice memos inside notes.
  • Audio Files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to discover and play local music files in the built-in ambient player and to allow selecting audio tracks for backup.
  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Allows NekoNote to trigger task alerts, scheduled reminders, and background backup progress notifications.
  • Biometric Lock (USE_BIOMETRIC): Enables fingerprint or face unlocking for protected notes. Verification is handled directly by Android's hardware-backed biometric security; NekoNote never sees or stores raw biometric data.
  • Nearby Devices & Wi-Fi: Used solely for the offline peer-to-peer QR/Radar note transfer feature between nearby devices without using the internet. No geolocation tracking is performed.

3. Google Sign-In and Google Drive Cloud Backup

NekoNote offers an optional cloud backup and restore feature powered by Google Sign-In and the Google Drive REST API:

  • Scope of Access: We request access strictly to the restricted Application Data folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata).
  • Isolation: NekoNote can only access its own dedicated hidden app folder in your Google Drive. NekoNote cannot view, access, edit, or delete any of your other personal files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Direct Connection: Backup files are encrypted in transit via HTTPS and uploaded directly between your device and Google's official cloud storage. We do not operate any intermediary servers.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: NekoNote's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

NekoNote offers an optional lifetime Pro license (nekonote_premium_lifetime) via the official Google Play Billing Library:

  • All payment processing is handled exclusively and securely by Google Play.
  • NekoNote never collects, handles, or stores your credit card numbers, banking credentials, or personal payment details.
  • We only receive an encrypted purchase token from Google Play to unlock Pro features across your devices.

5. Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

NekoNote uses standard, privacy-conscious libraries solely for core functionality:

  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition: Provides high-speed OCR text extraction. Image processing takes place entirely on your device with no cloud transmission.
  • Google Play In-App Review & Update API: Allows users to optionally review the app or receive in-app updates securely via Google Play.

6. Data Security and Privacy Protection

We implement multi-layered defenses to ensure your personal notes remain private:

  • PIN & Biometric Lock: Protect individual sensitive notes or lock the entire application.
  • Anti-Snoop Shield (FLAG_SECURE): Prevents unauthorized screen recording, screenshots, or task-switcher previews when viewing protected notes.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full and permanent control over your data:

  • Deleting a note or file inside the app removes it permanently from device storage.
  • Uninstalling NekoNote completely erases all local application databases and stored files.
  • You can delete your cloud backup at any time directly through the app's Google Drive Settings screen or by managing app permissions at Google Account Third-Party Connections.

8. Children's Privacy

NekoNote does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13. The application is a general productivity utility suitable for all audiences.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this URL with a revised effective date.
